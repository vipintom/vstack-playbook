# Independent Challenge

**Rule.** The author does not grade their own work.

Designs get attacked and deliveries get verified by a context that did not write them, or by a deliberate second pass with a different question.

**Trigger.** A design looks clean, or a delivery is about to be called complete.

**Test.** Did someone or something try to break it, and are the findings recorded?

**Failure mode.** Shared assumptions pass review and fail in production.
