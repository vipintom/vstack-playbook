# Procedures Over Vague Instructions

**Rule.** A skill is a procedure, not an encouragement.

If a step cannot say what to produce and where it goes, it is not a step.

**Trigger.** You are writing or following advice like "be careful" or "consider edge cases".

**Test.** Does each step name an artifact? Does the skill say when to stop?

**Failure mode.** Advice cannot be verified, so it cannot be completed or reviewed.
