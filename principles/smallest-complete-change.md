# Smallest Complete Change

**Rule.** Solve the stated problem, in the smallest change that fully solves it.

No speculative options, no future-proofing, no drive-by fixes.

**Trigger.** A change grows beyond the task that justified it.

**Test.** Can every line in the diff be traced to the outcome or its proof?

**Failure mode.** Unreviewable diffs and abstractions nobody asked for.
