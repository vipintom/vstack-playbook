# spike

**Applies when:** a decision needs an experiment, and the result may be thrown away.
**Not for:** production code (`build-feature`); a question reading can answer (`understand`).
**Budget:** 4 skill loads, one timebox.

## Steps

| # | Skill | Artifact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `shape` | the question and the decision it informs |
| 2 | `prototype` | the thinnest build that answers it, with a timebox |
| 3 | `evidence` | the measurement or observation, digested |
| 4 | `decisions` | the choice, alternatives and what the spike ruled out |

## Exit criteria

- The question is answered, or abandoned with the reason.
- The artifact is kept and labelled, or deleted deliberately.
- A decision record exists if the spike informed one.

## Without subagents

Build and measure in one session; keep the timebox visible in the focus detail.
Stop at the timebox even mid-build and report the partial result.
